NONPROFIT STATUS

Accountability & Transparency 

Flash Forward Foundation is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

TAX-EXEMPT DETAILS

  • Legal name: Flash Forward Foundation
  • EIN: 39-3605570
  • Location: Bellevue, Washington, United States
  • Tax-exempt status: 501(c)(3), effective August 5, 2025
  • Public charity classification: 509(a)(2)
  • IRS Publication 78: listed as eligible to receive tax-deductible contributions

IRS FEDERAL FORM 990

  • Fiscal year 2025: not filed yet. The first Form 990 will be posted here once it is filed. 

GIVING

  • Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. The online donation form is being set up on the donate page; to give now, write to the team.
