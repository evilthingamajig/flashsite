Engineering

This is an unpaid Engineering internship with Flash Forward Foundation. Work on solar study lights, improve build documentation, and help Builders assemble and check each light.

Apply for this track

Opens in Google Forms. Choose Engineering as your track.

What you could work on

  • Help prototype and test study-light components and designs.
  • Document assembly steps and quality checks.
  • Work with the team on practical improvements and clear technical guides.

A sample project

A documented design test or an illustrated assembly guide.

Projects are agreed with the team based on current needs, your interests, and the tools available.
