Marketing & Communications Internship for Students

Tell the story of a student-run nonprofit. You'll run social campaigns and write, design and make video for Flash Forward Foundation, with weekly feedback from a manager and monthly goals from experienced executives.

Resume

Application received

What you'll do

Social, writing, design and video

01

Run social campaigns

Plan and run campaigns on Flash Forward's social channels, then report what worked.

02

Write and design

Write posts, notes and stories, and design the graphics that go with them.

03

Make video

Shoot and edit short videos about building the lights and where they go, and grow the channel's views.

How it works

Three steps

Step 1

Onboarding

A short orientation on Flash Forward's mission and brand.

Step 2

Department course

Lessons, resources and practical assignments you do for the real organization.

Step 3

Projects

Complete at least two projects from a pool tied to your department's goals, with weekly reviews from your manager.

What you'll get

More than a line on a resume

01

Real, marketable skills

Social media, writing, design and video, applied to a real nonprofit's live campaigns.

02

Mentorship

Executives with professional backgrounds in marketing, growth, strategy and content set monthly goals; managers review work weekly.

03

Published work and service hours

Strong project work can be published with your permission as portfolio pieces, and Flash Forward signs off your volunteer service hours.

FAQ

Who can apply?

High school and college students.

Is the internship remote?

Yes. The internship is fully remote, so you can take part from anywhere.

Is it paid?

No. It is an unpaid internship with a student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

How much time does it take?

You tell us what you can give and the program flexes around your term. Managers review your work weekly, goals are set monthly, and interns complete at least two projects.

Can I earn service hours?

Yes. Flash Forward signs off volunteer service hours for intern work, including x2VOL for Bellevue School District students.

How do I apply?

Fill in the application on this page. It is a Google Form that takes a few minutes and asks for your resume.
