FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

A few answers about Flash Forward
and how you can be part of the work.

What does Flash Forward Foundation do?

We are a student-led nonprofit working to help students study after dark. We design solar study lights, work with assembly partners to build them, and place them through local distribution partners. See our impact.

How can my organization partner with you?

We work with organizations that can help build lights or place them with students. Tell us about your organization, location, and the kind of partnership you have in mind. You can also read about assembly partnerships and distribution partnerships.

How do I apply for an internship?

Use our internship application to tell us about your interests and experience. Internship applications have their own form, so you do not need to submit your application through this contact page.

Who decides which students receive the lights?

Our distribution partners know the schools and communities they serve. Each partner decides who receives a light. Flash Forward provides the lights to partners free of charge.

How can I support the work?

You can make a donation, start a fundraiser, or help us connect with a potential assembly or distribution partner.

Can I share a student or partner story?

Yes. Use the Share your story page to prepare an email for our team to review. Please get permission from anyone whose story or photo you include. Stories are reviewed before publication.

Where can I find your nonprofit information?

Flash Forward Foundation is a US 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 39-3605570. Our Nonprofit Status page has more information.
