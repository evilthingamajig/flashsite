About Flash Forward

What is Flash Forward Foundation?

A student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Bellevue, Washington. Student volunteers build $5.17 solar study lights and deliver them, through partner organizations, to students who have no electricity to study after dark.

How did Flash Forward get started?

In much of India and Sub-Saharan Africa the sun sets around 5:30–6 PM and school ends around 3 PM — leaving a student only 2–3 hours of daylight to study. Flash Forward was founded by students in Bellevue in 2024 to fix that: by students, for students. The IRS recognized us as a 501(c)(3) in August 2025; in 2026 we delivered 72 lights to partners in Haiti, India and DR Congo, and executives joined to build out our internship program.

Is Flash Forward a legitimate 501(c)(3)?

Yes — recognized by the IRS on August 5, 2025. Our EIN is 39-3605570.

Who will I be talking to when I write to you?

A student on the leadership team reads every message and replies by email.

The lights

What is a Flash Forward study light?

A small solar desk light: a printed case, a rechargeable battery, a charge board, LEDs and a solar panel, built by volunteers for $5.17 in parts.

How long does a charge last?

It recharges in about 7 hours of sunlight and gives up to about 12 hours of light on a full charge.

Who decides who receives a light?

The partner organization chooses recipients — it knows the students and communities it serves.

How do I request lights for my community?

Write to about@flashforwardfoundation.org and we’ll talk it through.

Giving

Can I choose where my light goes?

Yes — pick Haiti, India or DR Congo on the Sponsor a Light page, or let us choose where it’s needed most. We honour your preference.

Can I give monthly?

Yes — the Sponsor a Light page has a monthly option.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Feel free to reach out to us, and we’ll be happy to provide any details you need.

Internships

What is the Flash Forward internship?

A department-based program where you learn real, marketable skills — marketing, writing, design, partnerships, fundraising, research with mentorship from experienced executives and published project work.

How is an internship different from helping at builds?

Interns take a department course and own projects; you can also help with builds, events and workshops without taking the course.

How do I apply?

The same Google Form serves interns and anyone who wants to help; it takes a few minutes.

Who can apply?

High school and college students.
