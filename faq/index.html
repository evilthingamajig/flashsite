— The Study Light —

What is a Flash Forward study light?

+

A small solar panel, a rechargeable battery and two LEDs in a 3D-printed case. The light charges while the sun is up and provides reading light after sunset, without a wall outlet.

What is inside one light?

+

A 3D-printed case ($1.10), a 5V solar panel ($1.50), a LiPo battery ($1.65), a TP4056 charge board ($0.45), two 5 mm LEDs ($0.12) and a slide switch ($0.35). Parts come to $5.17 per light. The full list is on the study light page.

How long does a charge last?

+

The battery recharges in about 7 hours of sunlight and gives up to about 12 hours of light on a full charge. The light is built to last 1-2 years, and replacements are available to partners.

Who builds the lights?

+

Student volunteers print the case, assemble the parts and check that each light charges and turns on. Volunteers build without pay, so the $5.17 covers parts only. Shipping and a partner's distribution are separate. See how a light is built.

How many hours a day is a light used?

+

By our estimate, a light is used about 6 hours a day.

Why build a study light at all?

+

655 million people lived without electricity in 2024, according to Tracking SDG7: The Energy Progress Report 2026. The figure is context for why a small light is worth building.

— Where the Lights Go —

Where have the lights gone?

+

As of October 2026, Flash Forward has delivered 72 lights to three partners: 42 to Brighten Haiti in Haiti, 15 to Loksadhana in India and 15 to Malaika in the DR Congo. See where the lights go.

Who decides who receives a light?

+

The partner organization. Partners already work with students and families and know who needs a light and how to hand it over.

Can I choose where a light goes?

+

Yes. Tell the team your preference when you give on the donate page or through the contact form, and the team follows it when it can.

How do I request lights for my community?

+

Write to us through the contact form and choose the topic Request lights for my community, or email about@flashforwardfoundation.org.

— Donating —

How much does it cost to give a light?

+

Each light costs $5.17 in parts. Give on the donate page.

Where does the money go?

+

To parts for solar study lights: $5.17 per light. Volunteers build the lights without pay.

Is a gift tax-deductible?

+

To the extent allowed by law. Our donation processor emails a receipt for each gift. Tax status and the EIN are on the nonprofit status page.

Can I give monthly?

+

Yes. Monthly giving will be an option on the donate page when online giving opens.

— Volunteering and Internships —

How can I volunteer?

+

Volunteers build lights and keep the rest of the work moving: outreach, writing, fundraising and welcoming the next person. Say what you can do and how much time you have, and the team will answer. Start on the volunteer page.

What are the volunteer tracks?

+

Engineering and manufacturing, outreach and partnerships, communications, fundraising, volunteer coordination and STEM activities.

Is the internship paid?

+

No. The internship is unpaid and has no stipend. It is not a job offer at the end. Academic credit, if any, is between you and your school. See the internship page.

How does the application work?

+

There is no application portal and no posted deadline. Pick one track, then write your name, school, email, the term you want, how many hours a week you can give and the track. Send it on the application form or by email. A team member replies if the workshop can take a placement.

How is an internship different from volunteering?

+

Volunteers help with a task that is open. Interns have a named track, a term and a person to report to. Both are unpaid.

— About Flash Forward —

What is Flash Forward Foundation?

+

A student-run nonprofit in Bellevue, Washington. Volunteers build solar study lights so that students without electricity at home can keep studying after dark.

How did Flash Forward get started?

+

Students founded it in 2024 and built the first solar study light prototype that year. In 2025 the IRS recognized the organization as a public charity. In 2026 volunteers are building lights for partners in Haiti, India and the DR Congo.

Is Flash Forward a legitimate 501(c)(3) nonprofit?

+

Yes. The IRS recognizes Flash Forward Foundation as a 501(c)(3) public charity, effective August 5, 2025. The determination letter and the Publication 78 listing are posted on the nonprofit status page.

Who reads my message?

+

A student on the team. Write through the contact form or email about@flashforwardfoundation.org.

Still have a question?

We are a student-run team in Bellevue and a student reads every message.
