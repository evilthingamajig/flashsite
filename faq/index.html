Requesting Lights

Can I request lights for my school or organization?

Tell us about your organization, the students you work with, and their access to dependable lighting. Our team reviews distribution inquiries and follows up about the possibilities. An inquiry does not confirm a shipment, quantity, or delivery date.

Start a distribution inquiry

How it works

What is Flash Forward Foundation?

Flash Forward is a student-led US 501(c)(3) public charity founded in 2024 and based in Bellevue, Washington. We design solar study lights, work with Builders to assemble them, and deliver them through local distribution partners.

Read our story

How are students selected?

Distribution partners bring local knowledge and help identify students who need dependable lighting. Selection and delivery details are discussed with the partner rather than handled through an individual cash application.

How our model works

What does a student receive?

The program provides a solar study light free of charge through a distribution partner. The design combines a solar panel, rechargeable battery, LED, and printed housing. The program does not send cash payments to students.

Meet the study light

Is a study light a gift or a loan?

Students receive the lights free of charge. Our program is not a microloan or a product financing program.

Explore our approach

What are the lights intended for?

Our aim is to give students a dedicated source of light for reading and schoolwork after sunset where dependable lighting is difficult to access.

Why we focus on light

What do you know about student outcomes?

Our published figures describe recorded deliveries. We have not published a controlled study establishing changes in grades, study hours, health, or income. A personal story or delivery count should not be presented as proof of those outcomes.

How we report our work

How does the solar study light work?

The solar panel collects energy for charging, a rechargeable battery stores it, and the LED provides light. Engineering handles technical questions and design changes. Do not assume a runtime, certification, or weather rating that has not been verified for the current design.

Explore the design

Where we work

Where have you delivered lights?

As of October 2026, our recorded partner deliveries totaled 72 lights: 42 through Brighten Haiti, 15 through Loksadhana in India, and 15 through Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Personal hand-deliveries are kept separate from this partner total.

Explore our impact

What happens in the United States?

Flash Forward is based in Bellevue, Washington. Students and volunteer groups contribute to engineering, assembly, operations, fundraising, and communications. Our recorded partner deliveries are described on the Impact page.

Meet the team

Can you respond to an urgent request?

You can contact our team to explain a need. We cannot promise emergency response, an immediate shipment, or a delivery date through this website. Please wait for the team to confirm what is possible before making plans around a delivery.

Contact our team

How do communities take part?

Schools and community organizations can inquire about distribution. Schools, clubs, and makerspaces can also become Builders and help assemble lights. These are different roles, with their own coordination needs.

Working with Partners

Who are the study lights intended for?

The lights are intended for students who need dependable lighting for schoolwork after sunset. Distribution partners help identify students in their communities. We do not use this site to offer personal cash grants.

Learn about distribution

How do lights reach students?

Builders assemble and check lights, and the team coordinates delivery with a distribution partner. The partner helps identify students and arrange the local handoff. Timing and quantities need to be agreed for each delivery.

Follow the process

Do students need an account or phone?

There is no student account or cash-transfer signup on this website. A school or local organization can introduce a distribution inquiry to discuss needs and practical requirements with our team.

Start with a partner inquiry

How are Builders and distribution partners different?

Builders are assembly partners who make and check lights. Distribution partners help place lights with students. Corporate sponsors support agreed needs through funding, materials, or other contributions.

Explore Builders

How does a partnership inquiry work?

Tell us about your organization, the role you are interested in, and what you hope to contribute or request. The team reviews the inquiry and follows up. Do not purchase materials, schedule a build, or announce a shipment until the relevant details have been agreed.

Explore distribution partnerships

Do students pay for the lights?

Students receive the lights free of charge. If someone asks you to pay in order to be selected for a Flash Forward light, contact our team through the official contact page.

Contact Flash Forward

Why work through local organizations?

Schools and community organizations understand their students and local setting. Their knowledge helps guide selection, handoffs, and follow-up questions. Builders contribute a different kind of knowledge from assembling the light.

Our design-to-delivery model
