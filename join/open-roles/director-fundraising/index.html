Director of Fundraising

Lead Study Circle growth, sponsor asks, and grant research under board oversight.

What you’ll own

  • Plan monthly giving prompts tied to real program needs.
  • Coach Fundraising & Growth interns on ask quality.
  • Keep donor preference language accurate.
  • Brief the board on pipeline, not vanity metrics.

Who thrives here

Writers who can ask clearly and steward trust. Comfort talking about money without pressure tactics.

Entire organization is unpaid, including this seat. Expect async work, weekly check-ins, and direct board access.
