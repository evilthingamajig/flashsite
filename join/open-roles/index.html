Volunteer Leadership

Help a student-led team bring light to learning

Flash Forward Foundation leadership roles are unpaid. Directors help a student-led 501(c)(3) design and deliver solar study lights. Internships are a separate way to contribute.

See open roles

We’re hiring three directors

Each role is a volunteer, remote position. One application form covers all three; choose your role inside the form.

  • Director of Partnerships
  • Director of Marketing
  • Director of Fundraising

Director of Partnerships

  • Volunteer
  • Remote
  • Leads up to 10 interns
Apply for this role

You’ll own how Flash Forward’s solar study lights get built and delivered to students without reliable electricity.

What you’ll do

  • Manage our supply chain and resources.
  • Run assembly batches with Builders and clubs.
  • Keep the dashboard that tracks inventory, batches and deliveries up to date.
  • Qualify the schools and nonprofits that get lights to students.
  • Lead up to 10 interns.

What we look for

  • You’ve coordinated something with many moving parts and outside people.
  • You’ve reached an agreement with an organization you contacted.
  • You’ve run work from a tracker others relied on.
  • You’ve kept a people-focused project going over time.

Include with your application

  • Something you coordinated, with numbers: people, organizations, timeline, result.
  • A screenshot or link to a tracker or dashboard you ran.
  • A link to a long-running project you led.

We look for proof, not promises. We will teach you everything about how Flash Forward works, so focus on what you’ve already done. Applications without evidence can’t be considered.

Why join Flash Forward?

We connect student-led engineering, volunteer Builders, and local distribution partners around a practical goal: solar study lights for students who need dependable light after sunset.

The work spans four internship tracks: Partnerships & Operations, Fundraising & Growth, Marketing & Communications, and Engineering.

A volunteer leadership role means helping people work together, keeping responsibilities clear, and following through on agreed projects. Tell us about your interests and experience so we can discuss where you may fit.

1️⃣ Students First+

Our shared purpose is light for learning. Keep the students and the practical use of the light at the center of decisions.

💡 Practical Engineering+

Connect a useful design with the people, components, and guidance needed to build it.

⚡ Clear Responsibilities+

Make ownership, next steps, and commitments understandable to the people doing the work.

🌍 Local Knowledge+

Distribution partners understand their communities. Builders understand their assembly experience. Both perspectives matter.

What to Expect

Discuss the role, responsibilities, and availability with our team before committing.

🤝 Unpaid Volunteer Roles+

The leadership roles described here are volunteer opportunities. They do not include a salary or employee benefits. You can also explore the internship tracks.

📅 Agreed Availability+

Share your school, work, and other commitments so the team can discuss a realistic scope and schedule.

🧭 Defined Responsibilities+

Each role should have a clear purpose and an agreed set of responsibilities, rather than an open-ended promise to do everything.

💻 Remote Collaboration+

Much of the coordination can happen remotely. Location, communication needs, and any in-person work depend on the role.

📚 Learning Through Work+

Contribute to real projects and learn from the questions, decisions, and collaboration involved.

🔗 Connected Teams+

Engineering, operations, fundraising, and communications work together, with technical and partner commitments handled by the relevant owners.

Ask About Leadership Roles

Starting a Conversation

The exact steps depend on the opportunity. Our team can explain the current process when you inquire.

1. Introduce Yourself+

Tell us about your experience, interests, and the kind of work you want to contribute.

2. Discuss the Role+

Talk about the responsibilities, unpaid volunteer status, availability, and questions you have.

3. Explore the Fit+

The team may ask for relevant examples of your work or discuss a practical scenario for the role.

4. Meet the Team+

Discuss how the role would work with the people responsible for related projects.

5. Agree on Next Steps+

If there is a fit, confirm the scope and next steps before starting.

If you have a question about the application process or need to discuss access needs, contact about@flashforwardfoundation.org. Tell us which opportunity you are asking about so the right person can follow up.

Common FAQs

Do I need nonprofit experience?+

Relevant skills and follow-through matter. Tell us what you have worked on, what you want to learn, and how you hope to contribute. The requirements depend on the specific opportunity.

Are these paid positions?+

The leadership opportunities described here are unpaid volunteer roles. They do not include salary, relocation support, or employee benefits.

Ask the team about the specific role before applying so you understand the commitment.

What about availability and time zones?+

Share your location, time zone, school or work schedule, and the time you can reliably contribute. The team can then discuss the communication and coordination needs of the role.

Availability should be agreed with the team before you start, based on the work and your commitments. Ask about any scheduled meetings or deadlines during the conversation.
