Your path in three steps

Every stage happens online, and you hear back after each one.

Step 1

Take two free courses

Finish AI Fluency and Claude 101 before you submit. Both are free and self-paced, about 6.5 hours total.

AI Fluency → Claude 101 →

Want more practice? Flash Forward's Applied AI course is an optional extra.

Step 2

Submit your application

A short form about your background, plus two short answers: something you did for a community you belong to that nobody asked you to do, and a time you used AI to get something real done.

Reviewed as applications arrive. Apply by February 28, 2027.

Step 3

Interview and get matched

A take-home task using AI tools, a 25-minute recruiter call, then two 20-minute video interviews. If selected, you meet two to three host organizations.

Nothing technical to study for the interviews. Bring real examples.

Can I apply?

You can if all of these are true:

✓ You're 18 or older by the cohort start date

✓ You're authorized to work in the US without employer sponsorship

✓ You're at the start of your career, such as finishing school or a trade program, or leaving military service

✓ You already use AI tools in your day-to-day

And you'll need all three of these to apply:

1

Take AI Fluency and Claude 101

Finish both free courses and upload your certificates with your application.

2

Show something you've built

A website, app, automation, dashboard or AI project other people used. It doesn't need to be big, polished or from a job.

3

Bring community or nonprofit experience

Paid work, volunteering, a service program like AmeriCorps, or community organizing all count.

No degree, coding background or minimum years of experience required.

Where would I work?

At a nonprofit host, in person or hybrid. Cohort 3 includes some remote placements. We try to place you near home, and relocation support is available. Fellows must be open to relocation within the U.S. and be open to working on site, since remote matches aren't guaranteed.

Who would employ me?

Flash Forward, as a full-time W-2 employee, with Anthropic funding the program.

I applied for an earlier cohort. Can I apply again?

Yes. Submit a new application for Cohort 3.

Can current students apply?

Yes, as long as the fellowship won't require you to pause your studies.

Anthropic and Flash Forward co-design the program strategy. Flash Forward is the program architect and employer of record, responsible for recruiting, screening, training, and employing every fellow.

Flash Forward brings nine years of experience preparing engineers for the most competitive technical roles in the country. Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that builds reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems, like Claude.

That infrastructure (curriculum, employer relationships, and training at scale) is what makes Internship tracks work in partnership with Anthropic.

Everything you need to know about Internship tracks

Internship tracks is a launchpad for people at the very start of their careers interested in mission-driven work. It's a fit if you're:

• Finishing or just finished a degree or trade program, and haven't yet held a full-time role

• Transitioning out of military service into your first civilian career

• In your first full-time job and looking to move into mission-driven work

This fellowship is built around being a first step.

Yes. Fellows are full-time W-2 Flash Forward employees with salary and benefits for the full 12 months of the fellowship.

Flash Forward is the program architect and employer of record. We recruit and screen fellows, design the AI-native curriculum, manage training throughout the fellowship year, and handle all employment administration.

Applications are open for Cohort 3, which starts in August 2027. Apply by February 28, 2027. Applications are reviewed as they arrive. Apply now →
