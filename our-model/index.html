Study Unlock Pathway

  • Design

    Light after dark → homework finished → study hours unlocked.

    Every placement receives a Study Light — a student-led flashlight unit that unlocks after-dark study time. Partners assemble and distribute; Flash Forward measures study hours as the primary KPI.

  • Assemble

    From Study Light to study hours.

    We provide practical study-hour tracking that starts with savings from kerosene. Families learn to use their Study Lights effectively and reinvest their savings into sustainable assets, like livestock, that multiply over time.

  • Deliver

    Light doesn’t just change lives. It opens doors to new opportunities.

    Study Lights and financial knowledge allow families to choose their future. Savings fund education, businesses, or opportunity. Recipients report experiencing dignity, freedom, and self-worth—feeling empowered to break cycles of poverty and pursue new opportunities.

  • Unlock

    Local leaders. Global change.

    Our model grows through student leadership and partner networks. Assembly partners build Study Lights; distribution partners place them with students; Flash Forward tracks study hours.
