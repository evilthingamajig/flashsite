Why Light?

When the sun sets, darkness steals education, safety, and income.


Solar light brings it all back.

THE FASTEST PATH TO THE BIGGEST IMPACT

Families living in ultra-poverty are forced to spend up to 30% of their income on dangerous, non-renewable energy sources in order to light their homes after dark.

Flash Forward’s combination of our patented solar light and a specialized financial education empowerment program enables families to reclaim that 30% and reinvest it in livestock, education, and business development.

By addressing this pressing and costly need and providing guidance for a new way to manage their household finances, families experience not just an immediate improvement in their standard of living but are also able to change the trajectory of their future for generations to come.

Entire communities transition from survival to self-reliance.

LIGHT IMPACTS EVERYTHING

The Flash Forward Lighthouse Program does more than just help families save money.

  • Education

    Light unlocks learning.

    When the sun sets, studying usually stops. Solar lights provide children with safe, reliable light to read, write, and dream after dark.

    More hours mean more opportunity and brighter futures within reach.

  • Health & Safety

    No more kerosene. No more danger.

    Solar lights replace toxic kerosene, removing the daily dangers of fire, burns, and harmful smoke.

    Families breathe cleaner air, avoid deadly accidents, and finally feel safe in their own homes at night.

  • Women

    From vulnerable to powerful.

    A solar light gives women control of their time, income, and choices. Combined with financial literacy, it becomes a launchpad for dignity, independence, and protection for their children.

    Watch “Ukwati (The Wedding),” a powerful film on child marriage and the impossible choices mothers face in extreme poverty.

  • Environment

    One light. Massive climate impact.

    To date, our solar lights have reduced emissions by up to 230 million tons of CO₂ a year.

    They also reduce black carbon, a major driver of climate change.

    Clean energy creates a brigther future.

Solar Light Design

Flash Forward’s patented technology provides sustainable, renewable light to households in the most impoverished communities in the world. 

Our solar lights are designed for real-life use. They can be worn on the body, on the head, or used hands-free—giving families the flexibility to carry out daily tasks safely after dark.

Compact, portable, water-resistant, and durable, the lights are built to meet the demands of the people we serve. Whether farming, fishing, or fetching water, families can keep their light accessible and secure.

Every feature is inspired by the people we serve. Flash Forward continuously learns from light recipients to improve our designs, ensuring each innovation truly meets their needs.

Our lights have even been recognized by JerryRigEverything, a tech reviewer with 8.5 million YouTube followers, for their durability and practical design.

solar Light Features

  • Small and Lightweight: Easy to transport or conceal 

  • Long-Lasting Battery: Lasts up to 120 hours of light

  • Multi-Purpose Lanyard: Allows for light to be portable for various needs: headlamp, on the body, or in the house.

  • Various Light Settings: Provides multiple light options: dim to bright light, flashing emergency

  • Raised Heart Power Button: Easy to find for those with poor eyesight or in complete darkness 

  • Delayed Power Button: The light does not accidentally turn on and drain the battery 

  • Large Operational Click Button: Inclusive for all recipients without the use of fingers or control of fine motor skills

  • Multi-Functional Packaging: Slit on top of the box to function as a bank for savings  

  • Micro USB: Allows for a quicker charge if electricity is available  

  • Raised Corners: A layer of protection for the solar panel in rough conditions 

  • UL Certification: Verifies the safety and longevity of the lithium battery
