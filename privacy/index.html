This page explains how Flash Forward Foundation collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to our website, flashforwardfoundation.org.

At Flash Forward Foundation, we take your privacy seriously. By using or accessing our website in any manner, you acknowledge that you accept the practices described below. Your use of the website is also subject to our Terms of Service, which incorporates this Privacy Policy by reference.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, topic, and message when you submit a form. When you donate, our donation processor, Givebutter, collects and processes the payment and billing information needed to complete the transaction and may provide us with donation records. Flash Forward Foundation does not directly collect or store full payment-card details.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your message, process internship applications, keep you updated about our programs, and operate, protect, and improve our website and mission. We do not sell your personal information.

How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with service providers who help us operate our website and programs (such as our form-processing and donation partners), when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger or transfer of assets. We may also share aggregated or de-identified information.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal and tax obligations as a nonprofit, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Forms & Third-Party Processors

Contact forms are delivered through FormSubmit, a third-party form service. Internship applications may use Google Forms. Donations are processed by Givebutter. These providers handle information under their own privacy notices and security practices.

Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. You can control cookies through your browser settings and opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

International Users

Flash Forward Foundation is based in the United States. Information you provide will be processed and stored in the United States. If you access our website from outside the United States, you consent to that transfer.

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal. Because there is no common industry standard for how to respond, our website does not currently respond differently based on a Do Not Track signal.

Children's Privacy

Flash Forward Foundation was founded by students, and our interns are students themselves. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental involvement. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can remove it.

Data Security

We seek to protect your personal information using reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures. No method of transmitting or storing data over the internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask what information we have about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, to the extent required by applicable law.

Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Flash Forward Foundation is not liable for unauthorized access to or use of your information, or for the privacy or security practices of third-party services we use or link to, except as required by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated Policy.

Contact Us

Questions: about@flashforwardfoundation.org.
