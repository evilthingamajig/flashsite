Sponsor a Study Light

Each solar study light costs $5.17 in parts. Choose how many lights to sponsor, give once or monthly, and finish checkout on Every.org. Student volunteers build the lights for free.

Checkout is on Every.org, and one light is $5.17 in parts. Every.org emails your receipt. For tax status and documents, see nonprofit status.

Ways to donate:

  • Sponsor Lights Online (Every.org). Use the box above. Choose 1, 5, 10 or 25 lights, give once or monthly, and finish checkout on Every.org. One light is $5.17 in parts.
  • Dedicate a Light. You can give a light in honor or in memory of someone. Write first and the team will say what recognition it can actually offer. Ask about a dedication.
  • Donate Equipment. Ask before you ship or drop anything off. The workshop can look at 3D printers and filament, soldering irons and stations, solar panels and cells, rechargeable batteries and chargers, LEDs, wiring and components, multimeters and hand tools, and laptops for CAD. Offer equipment.
  • Receipts. Every.org emails the receipt. Whether a gift is tax-deductible, and the organization's tax documents, are on the nonprofit status page and in the questions.

Contact Us!

For questions about gifts, dedications or equipment, write to the Flash Forward team.
