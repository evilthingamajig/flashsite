FAQ

Questions

What does a gift cover?

$5.17 covers the parts for one light. Volunteers build for free; shipping is separate.

Can I choose where my light goes?

Yes — pick a partner country above, or let Flash Forward choose where it's needed most. Flash Forward honours the preference.

Is it tax-deductible?

To the extent allowed by law. Feel free to reach out and we'll be happy to provide any details you need.

Flash Forward
Foundation

A student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Bellevue, Washington. Volunteers build $5.17 solar study lights for students who have no electricity to study after dark.
