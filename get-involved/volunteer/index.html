HELP US BUILD LIGHTS

Volunteers build solar study lights and keep the rest of the work moving: outreach, writing, fundraising, and welcoming the next person. Say what you can do and how much time you have. The team will answer with what is actually open.

WAYS YOU CAN SUPPORT

Volunteering has several tracks. Pick the one that fits what you can do, or choose more than one on the form below.

Engineering
and Manufacturing

CAD, 3D printing, soldering, assembly, and testing, depending on the current workshop and your experience.

Outreach
and Partnerships

Research schools, sponsors, and nonprofit partners, and support outreach the team has approved.

Communications
and Fundraising

Draft posts and captions, research grants, and help with community fundraisers the team has approved.

Volunteer Coordination
and STEM Activities

Welcome new people and match them with an open task, or help lead a hands-on activity when a supervised session is scheduled.

READY TO VOLUNTEER?

Fill out the form below to tell the team who you are and what you would like to do. A person on the team will write back. There is no portal and no posted deadline.
