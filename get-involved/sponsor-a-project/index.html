Sponsor a study light project for students who cannot count on electricity after dark. The gift funds lights that assembly and distribution partners place. Gifts are preference-based. Placement is best effort.

How sponsoring a project works

Sponsoring a study light project should be straightforward. Here are three steps you can expect:

We place your gift where students need light

After you sponsor, the gift funds study lights. Assembly partners build them and distribution partners place them with students.

Covered Costs

We share updates from the work

While lights are being assembled and placed, we share updates and stories from the partners doing the work.

You see where lights went

When a placement is complete, we share an update on the project: where the lights went and what students can do with them. Placement is best effort.

Featured programs

Flash Forward places study lights with students in Haiti, India, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. These programs show how a sponsorship becomes lights students can use.

Distribution partners place study lights with students who do not have reliable electricity after school. A sponsorship funds that placement. The partner, not a map pin, decides who receives a light.

How a sponsorship is used

Assembly partners build the study lights. Distribution partners carry them to students. Flash Forward is a student-led 501(c)(3). Gifts are preference-based, and placement is best effort.
