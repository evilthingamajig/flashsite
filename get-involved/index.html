Get Involved

Flash Forward is a student-run nonprofit in Bellevue, Washington. We build solar study lights that cost $5.17 in parts so students without electricity can study after dark. There are simple steps you can take right now to help, and all of them start with a message or a gift.

Start Your Own Fundraiser

Every light starts as a $5.17 set of parts. A class, a club or a family can cover a batch of lights together.

Write to the team and we will help you plan a community fundraiser for lights.

Your Impact

What a gift covers. One light costs $5.17 in parts: a 3D-printed case, a solar panel, a battery, a charge board, two LEDs and a switch. Student volunteers print and assemble every light for free.

Where the lights are. Volunteers have delivered 72 lights to three partners: 42 to Brighten Haiti, 15 to Loksadhana in India and 15 to Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

What a light does. It charges in daylight and gives reading light after dark. By our estimate, a student can study about 6 hours a day with one light.
