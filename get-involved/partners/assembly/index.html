Frequently asked questions

What if I want to make a multi-year commitment?

That’s great! Some donors and corporate partners do. You’ll collaborate with our philanthropy team to create a giving plan that aligns with your goals.

Email matthew@newstoryhomes.org for more information.

Where can I learn more about your recent impact?

Check out our most recent Annual Report to see the impact we've made in the past year.

Does your work align with any of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals?

Yes — housing touches 13 of the 17 SDGs. Our land development strategy directly addresses SDGs 1 (No Poverty), 5 (Gender Equality), 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), and 10 (Reduced Inequalities). View our Strategy to learn more.
