What is the Flash Forward Internship?

The internship is for a high school or college student who can work with the team in Bellevue. You take one track for a term you propose, and someone on the leadership team is your contact for that placement. The tracks cover engineering and manufacturing, outreach and partnerships, communications, fundraising, volunteer coordination, and STEM activities.

The internship is unpaid and is not a job offer. Placements follow what the workshop can supervise.

APPLY TODAY

Watch a volunteer assemble a Flash Forward study light.

WHAT THE PLACEMENT GIVES YOU
  • One named track, chosen from the six the team runs.
  • A term that you propose.
  • A person on the leadership team who is your contact for the placement.
  • Hands-on work with the student team in Bellevue.
  • Before you start, the name of the person you report to.
WHO CAN APPLY
  • A high school or college student.
  • Able to work with the team in Bellevue.
  • Able to say which one track you want and how many hours a week you can give.
HOW TO APPLY
  1. Pick one track.
  2. Write your name, school, email, the term you want, how many hours a week you can give, and the track.
  3. Send it on the application form or by email.

If your school needs a form signed, send the form with your application.

THE TRACKS
  • Engineering and manufacturing. CAD, 3D printing, soldering, assembly, testing, and design notes, depending on the current workshop and your experience
  • Outreach and partnerships. Research schools, sponsors, and nonprofit partners, and support outreach the team has approved
  • Communications. Draft posts, captions, and short explanations of the work
  • Fundraising. Grant research, community fundraisers, and donor notes the team has approved
  • Volunteer coordination. Welcome new people and match them with an open task
  • STEM activities. When a supervised session is on the calendar, help lead a hands-on activity about the light
WHAT IT IS NOT
  • It is unpaid. There is no stipend.
  • It is not a job offer at the end.
  • Academic credit is between you and your school.
  • There is no posted deadline and no application portal.
