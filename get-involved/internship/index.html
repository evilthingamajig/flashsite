Internships

Do real work for a real nonprofit.

Interns work alongside experienced executives on live campaigns and leave with real, marketable skills and published work.

ApplyHow it works

What you'll get

More than a line on a resume

Real, marketable skills

Marketing, writing, design, partnerships, fundraising and research, applied to a real nonprofit's live work.

Mentorship

Executives with professional backgrounds in marketing, growth, strategy and content set monthly goals; managers review work weekly.

Published work

Strong project work can be published with your permission, as real portfolio pieces.

How it works

Three steps

Step 1

Onboarding

A short orientation on Flash Forward's mission and brand.

Step 2

Department course

Lessons, resources and practical assignments you do for the real organization.

Step 3

Projects

Complete at least two projects from a pool tied to department goals (for example, grow video engagement, run a social campaign, build a partner-research database or draft a grant proposal).

FAQ

Who can apply?

High school and college students.

How much time does it take?

You tell us what you can give and the program flexes around your term. Managers review your work weekly, goals are set monthly, and interns complete at least two projects.

How do I apply?

Use the same Google Form for internships and other ways to help; it takes a few minutes.
