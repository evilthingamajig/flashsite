How to write a high school resume with no experience

A high school resume with no experience is built from what you already have: school, activities, volunteering, projects and skills. Describe each one with a strong verb and a specific result, keep it to one page, and add real experience as soon as you can.

How to get an internship in high school, step by step

To get an internship in high school, decide what kind of work you want to try, ask the people and organizations closest to you first, send short and specific emails, and follow up once. Nonprofits, including remote ones, are often the most open to high school students.

Student guide

How to put volunteer work on a resume (and internships too)

Put volunteer work and unpaid internships in your Experience section if they involved real responsibilities, formatted just like a job: role, organization, dates and two to four specific bullets. Use a separate Volunteer section only if you have plenty of paid work already.

How to start a nonprofit as a student

To start a nonprofit as a student, begin with a project or club, sharpen your mission, build a team with adult advisors, and only then move to a fiscal sponsor or incorporation and 501(c)(3) recognition. Joining a student-run nonprofit first is a strong alternative.
