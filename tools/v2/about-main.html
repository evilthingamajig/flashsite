Our story

Flash Forward Foundation was founded in 2024 by students in Bellevue, Washington, on a simple belief: studying shouldn’t stop when the sun goes down.

About 655 million people live without electricity. For a student, that means the school day ends at sunset.

So we build for the hours after dark.

We design a solar study light that costs $5.17 in parts. Robotics clubs and makerspaces assemble it, and local partners hand it to the students who need it.

Students run the organization, from partnerships to fundraising, and nobody here is paid.

Our journey so far

Flash Forward started with a few students and one prototype. Here is how it grew.

Founded by students in Bellevue.

A group of students in Bellevue, Washington started Flash Forward and built the first prototype of the solar study light.

Recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity.

The IRS recognized Flash Forward Foundation in August 2025. That December, students in Dumas, Haiti wrote essays about what solar power changed at their school.

72 study lights delivered in three countries.

Lights reached students through Brighten Haiti, Loksadhana in India and Malaika in the DR Congo. Executives joined to build out our departments and the internship program.
