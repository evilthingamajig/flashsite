How sponsoring works

  1. Give on the donate page and pick the fund for the partner you want to support, or leave it on “where it’s needed most”.
  2. We buy the parts and send them to an assembly partner, who builds the lights.
  3. The lights ship to the distribution partner, who hands them to students.
  4. When a shipment is delivered, we write it up in News.

What a gift covers

We count gifts in study lights. One light costs $5.17 in parts, so $51.70 covers ten and $258.50 covers fifty.

Shipping is worked out with each partner. Sometimes the partner covers it and sometimes we do.

Nobody at Flash Forward is paid, from interns to the board.

FAQ

Will my gift go exactly where I choose?

We treat your choice as a preference and do our best to honor it. Lights ship to partners in batches, so we cannot promise that a gift matches one shipment exactly.

Who decides which students get a light?

The distribution partner. They know the students and the school, so they choose.

Is my gift tax deductible?

Flash Forward Foundation is a 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 39-3605570. Gifts are deductible to the extent the law allows, and Givebutter emails your receipt.

Can a company or a school sponsor a project?

Yes. Write to about@flashforwardfoundation.org and tell us what you have in mind.
