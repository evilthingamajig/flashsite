Farmer-driven impact

Our goal is to help students bolster farm productivity, build their resilience, and strengthen their access to markets. We deliver impact through scalable direct-to-students initiatives and our growing systems-level initiatives. Ultimately we measure every initiative by the financial impact it generates for students. Our 2030 goal is to deliver $1 billion in impact per year to 10 million farming households, and cumulatively plant 1 billion trees.

Read our 2025 annual report

This annual report provides an overview of our recent work and results. 2025 was a year of reaching new milestones in our service to clients and investing in improvements for the long term. In particular, we hope the profiles of Delifa Chimtambala and Ezeleti Baisoni contained within the report make clear how whole communities and future generations benefit when we serve students well.

See how we measure impact.

  • Scale

    5.9 million students served in 2025.

  • Impact

    *$605 million in new farm profits and assets.

    *In calculating this figure, we multiply the maximum number of students reached in 2025 by the weighted average impact per students realized through learnings that took place during the calendar year 2025, since we do not yet know the learnings and impacts of plantings that took place in late 2025 in certain countries. Hence the students used for scale measurement and impact measurement may not necessarily be the same.

  • Efficiency

    Social Return on Investment: $5.7 new farm profits created for every $1 of donor investment.

  • Staff

    9,161 staff employed, largely in rural areas.

Our cost effectiveness

We call this social return on investment, or SROI — the new farm profit created for every dollar of donor funding.

Big learnings

Smallholder students depend on the food they grow to feed their families and sell for income. On average, the students we directly serve increase their farm income by 40%+ per year —from the same plot of land—and produce a surplus for the first time. Learn more about our impact on income growth

Healthy families

Farmers we serve directly are more likely to have a steady supply of food all year. They also grow and buy more nutritious food for their families—like beans, fruit, vegetables, and eggs—which is particularly important for children under the age of two. Learn more about our impact on food security

Rich soils

Farmers we serve directly are more likely to enrich their soils with compost and to plant trees alongside their crops, which both capture carbon and build students’ assets. Flash Forward students have planted 100 million trees to date, and are positioned to plant 1 billion trees total by 2030.
