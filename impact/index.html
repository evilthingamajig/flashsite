Our Impact

Student volunteers build $5.17 solar study lights for students who study after dark. Here is the program record as of October 2026.

72
lights delivered to partners
3
countries: Haiti, India, DR Congo
~432
estimated study hours a day
~100
students in our solar workshops

655 million people lived without electricity in 2024 (Tracking SDG7, 2026).

Brighten Haiti student essays

In their own words

Students at Institution Sainte Bernadette Soubirous de Dumas, a school in Dumas, Haiti served by Brighten Haiti, wrote about school before and after solar power. Shown in English translation.

“I can charge my phone now, I feel comfortable, and I'm no longer embarrassed.”
Mitchcaila, 7th grade, Dumas, HaitiView the essay →
“When it was hot, we had no electricity for the fans, so we had to make wind with notebook pages or hand fans.”
Saintina, 8th grade, Dumas, HaitiView the essay →
“A place without electricity is like a machine without an engine.”
Milanda, 6th grade, Dumas, HaitiView the essay →

Essays by students at Institution Sainte Bernadette Soubirous de Dumas, written for a Brighten Haiti essay contest (English translation).
