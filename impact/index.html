Our Impact

Student volunteers build $5.17 solar study lights for students who study after dark. Here is the program record as of October 2026.

72
lights delivered to partners
3
countries: Haiti, India, DR Congo
~432
estimated study hours a day
~100
students in our solar workshops

655 million people lived without electricity in 2024 (Tracking SDG7, 2026).
