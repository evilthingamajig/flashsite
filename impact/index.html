How change starts

From a workbench
to a school night.

Students build the idea.

Our student-led team develops a practical solar study light. Assembly partners bring the design to life with parts, tools, and care.

Local partners make the connection.

Schools and community organizations identify where lights can help and choose the students who receive them, free of charge.

Learning carries on.

A light charges in the sun and can be used after dark. Students and partners help us understand how it fits into everyday learning.

Meet the communities behind the work

What we’re working toward

More room
for possibility.

  • Time to learn

    A portable light gives students an option for reading and homework when daylight or reliable electricity is limited.

  • Energy from the sun

    A rechargeable solar design puts a useful renewable-energy tool into students’ hands.

  • Students helping students

    The work brings design, teamwork, and problem-solving together around a shared educational purpose.

Read our published record
What we can count

Our October 2026 record lists 42 lights delivered through Brighten Haiti, 15 through Loksadhana, and 15 through Malaika. These are delivery figures, rather than measured improvements in learning.

What students can tell us

Stories can describe when a light is used, what makes it useful, and what could work better. Partners and students can share these experiences through our story submission page.

What we still want to understand

We want to understand changes in study time and students’ experience over time. We haven’t published measured learning outcomes, so we keep those goals separate from our delivery totals.
