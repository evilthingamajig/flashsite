Our impact

Every light we've built is with a student

Student interns build $5.17 solar study lights and partner organizations place them with students who study after dark.

Program record as of October 2026

Workshops

Teaching solar energy

About 100 students have taken part in 4 hands-on workshops where the team teaches how solar charging works and how the study light is assembled.

Read the workshops note →

655 million

people lived without electricity in 2024

Tracking SDG7: The Energy Progress Report 2026 (IEA, IRENA, UN Statistics Division, World Bank, WHO)

In much of India and Sub-Saharan Africa school ends around 3 PM and the sun sets around 6 PM — leaving a student only 2–3 hours of daylight to study before the evening goes dark. A charged study light adds about 6 hours of reading time a night.
