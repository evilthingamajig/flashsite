Our impact

Every light we've built is with a student

As of October 2026, Flash Forward Foundation has delivered 72 solar study lights to three partners: 42 to Brighten Haiti, 15 to Loksadhana in India and 15 to Malaika in the DR Congo. By our estimate that adds about 432 study hours a day, and about 100 students have joined our solar workshops.

Workshops

Teaching solar energy

About 100 students have taken part in 4 hands-on workshops where the team teaches how solar charging works and how the study light is assembled.

Read the workshops note →

655 million

people lived without electricity in 2024

Tracking SDG7: The Energy Progress Report 2026 (IEA, IRENA, UN Statistics Division, World Bank, WHO)

In much of India and Sub-Saharan Africa school ends around 3 PM and the sun sets around 6 PM — leaving a student only 2–3 hours of daylight to study before the evening goes dark. A charged study light adds about 6 hours of reading time a night.

Key facts

  • Flash Forward Foundation was founded in 2024 by students in Bellevue, Washington. (Source: Founded by students)
  • The IRS recognized Flash Forward Foundation as a 501(c)(3) public charity effective August 5, 2025 (EIN 39-3605570). (Source: IRS determination letter)
  • As of October 2026, Flash Forward has delivered 72 solar study lights: 42 to Brighten Haiti, 15 to Loksadhana (India) and 15 to Malaika (DR Congo). (Source: Program record)
  • Each light costs $5.17 in parts, recharges in about 7 hours of sunlight and gives up to about 12 hours of light. (Source: The Study Light)
  • By our estimate, the 72 lights add about 432 study hours a day, assuming about 6 hours of use per light. (Source: Program record)
  • About 655 million people lived without electricity in 2024. (Source: Tracking SDG7: The Energy Progress Report 2026)
