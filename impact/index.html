Our Impact

Student volunteers build $5.17 solar study lights for students who study after dark. Here is the program record, as it stands.

Where the Lights Go

Many students have no electricity at home to study after dark. A small solar light can help.

As of October 2026, our program record shows:

72

lights delivered to three partners

~432

estimated learning hours per day across 72 lights

3

partners in Haiti, India and the DR Congo

in parts for one light, built by volunteers for free

~100

students reached through 4 STEM workshops

Where the lights went

All 72 lights have been delivered to three partner organizations. The full record and map are on Where the Lights Go.
Brighten Haiti, Haiti: 42 lights

Solar power for schools, hospitals and families across Haiti through Solar4Schools.

Read More
Loksadhana, India: 15 lights

A charitable trust in Maharashtra advancing rural education, women’s empowerment and health.

Read More
Malaika, DR Congo: 15 lights

A free school for girls in Kalebuka, near Lubumbashi, with health and community programs.
