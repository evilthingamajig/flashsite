Nonprofit Status & Reporting

Flash Forward Foundation is a student-led US 501(c)(3) public charity based in Bellevue, Washington. Our EIN is 39-3605570. Here you can find our IRS documents and the updates that explain our work.

Know the work your support makes possible.

We want supporters to understand what has been completed, what is planned, and what a published figure actually measures.

As of October 2026, our recorded partner deliveries totaled 72 study lights: 42 through Brighten Haiti, 15 through Loksadhana in India, and 15 through Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Delivery records are different from measured outcomes. Our impact overview and Stories hub provide context; they do not establish changes in grades, health, or income.

Our IRS determination letter and Publication 78 listing are linked below. For questions about financial records or a document you need, contact about@flashforwardfoundation.org.
