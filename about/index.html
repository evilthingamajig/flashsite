Our story

We’re students building
a brighter way to learn.

A simple belief brought us together: a student’s opportunity to learn shouldn’t depend on access to electricity.

An idea made tangible

Start with a problem.
Build something useful.

A small solar panel, a rechargeable battery, and a light. A practical idea that students can build together and put to work beyond the classroom.

Where we began

One idea in Bellevue.
A purpose we share.

Flash Forward began in 2024, when students in Bellevue, Washington, set out to build a solar study light. We wanted to work on a problem students could understand firsthand: needing time and a place to learn.

When reliable electricity is out of reach, a school night can depend on the daylight that remains. We saw a way to bring our interest in technology together with something useful: a portable light that charges in the sun and can be used after dark.

The first prototype was a beginning. The work has grown to include students taking responsibility for design, partnerships, fundraising, and communications, with mentors helping them learn along the way. Flash Forward became a recognized 501(c)(3) public charity in August 2025.

Built together.
Connected by local partners.

A light needs more than a good design to become useful. Assembly partners help build the lights; distribution partners connect them with students. The schools and organizations we work with know their communities and choose who receives them.

Through Brighten Haiti, Loksadhana in India, and Malaika in the DR Congo, our October 2026 program record documents 72 study lights delivered. Those partnerships are how work at a student’s workbench reaches another student’s school night.

What guides us

Light for learning.
Opportunity to contribute.

We work to reduce energy inequality and expand educational opportunity through solar study lights.

Along the way, students gain the chance to contribute to real work, learn from mentors, and see how their skills can serve others.

The next chapter

The story belongs to
everyone who helps write it.

It belongs to the students who design and assemble, the partners who make introductions and deliveries, and the students who put a light to use. Their experiences help us understand what should come next.

We’re creating space for those voices. Students, schools, organizations, and assembly partners can share a story with our team for review and publication.
