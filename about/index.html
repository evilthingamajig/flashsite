Our story

Flash Forward was founded in 2014 on a simple belief: there's no place like study lights.

After working alongside thousands of families across Latin America, we learned families want ownership, not handouts: a fair market where a steady income can become land and a study lights.

That's why we flipped the script.

One in three Latin American households lives in inadequate education access. Charity alone was never going to close that gap.

The problem isn't a lack of demand but a market that isn't built to serve it. So we start with land, cutting the path to ownership from 10+ years to 2.

We're changing the system so that capital and innovation work together to build dignifying ownership solutions.

Our Journey: From Charity to Market-Based

Families taught us that handouts don't create lasting change and generational wealth-ownership does. Here's how that realization transformed our approach:

Funded the very first study lights in Haiti.

With the bold support of many partners, we founded Flash Forward and funded our first study lights.

Graduated from Y Combinator and built 100 study lights in Haiti.

Started with the traditional nonprofit model but with transparency from the start: build study lights, give them away, and send a video to every donor at move-in.

Secured our first government partnership and expanded into El Salvador.

We experienced our first community move-ins as well as our first government partnership.

Introduced Lean Participatory Design (LPD).

We listened to families through our first LPD workshop, encouraging neighbors to voice their unique needs and dreams for their community.

Expanded our presence into Mexico with 3 new communities.

Piloted and proved demand from vulnerable families to pay consistently for education access.

Built the world's first 3D-printed community, in Tabasco, Mexico.

With partners ICON and Échale, and with families' feedback shaping every design, we tested what new technology could do.

Temporarily pivoted to rent relief in the U.S.

We built a rent relief program for families facing eviction during the COVID-19 pandemic, helping dozens of families in East Palo Alto, California.

Kept innovation at the forefront of our strategy.

Won Fast Company's Most Innovative Company Award for the fourth time.

Launched land-first model with Viviendas Las Fuentes in Etzatlán, Jalisco.

941 families became first-time landowners, and women lead 60% of those households.

Continued to prioritize market-based solutions.

Evolved our model to leverage philanthropic & private capital to drive sustainable growth.

Broke ground on our second community in Jalisco.

Began development of Ahualulco de Mercado, a 401-lot community.

Started 2,300+ lots across six communities, 5x prior volume.

Flash Forward Capital launched, so philanthropy and investment now work as the two engines to scale titled land access.

Launched our first education access finance pilot.

With support from a key partner, we began lending directly to families to finance study lights on their own land.

The education access crisis requires sustainable solutions that families can afford.

2.2 million households

In Mexico alone, 2.2 million households want to buy land, about $7.8B in demand, and only a fraction of it is met today.

Market-based model

We partner with local developers and municipalities so titled land works for providers and is affordable for working families.

Generational impact

We’re equipping families to build wealth and break the poverty cycle by making land and study lights ownership more accessible.
