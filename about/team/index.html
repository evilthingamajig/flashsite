The Flash Forward team

The people behind
the light.

For students, by students

Different skills.
A shared purpose.

We bring students and mentors together around a practical goal: helping students without reliable electricity keep learning after dark.

Some of us design solar study lights. Others build partnerships, tell our story, or raise the funds that keep projects moving. Together with our assembly and distribution partners, we connect that work to the students it is meant to serve.

Meet the people behind the work

Learn by doing

Real projects give students room to build practical skills, take responsibility, and learn alongside teammates and mentors.

Build together

Engineering, partnerships, fundraising, and communications all play a part. We work across teams to turn ideas into study lights.

Keep students at the center

A light is a tool for learning. The students who use it, and the partners who know their communities, give our work its purpose.
