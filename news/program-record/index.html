Note · October 6, 2026 · Bellevue, Washington

Program record: 72 lights for three partners

42 lights have shipped to Haiti. Thirty more are allocated to partners in India and the DR Congo and are not described as shipped.

What the record shows

As of October 6, 2026, Flash Forward has built 72 solar study lights for partners in three countries.

Forty-two of those lights have shipped to Brighten Haiti. That is the only shipment this record treats as confirmed.

Forty-two of those lights have shipped to Brighten Haiti. That is the only shipment this record treats as confirmed.

Allocated, not shipped

Fifteen lights are built for the partnership with Loksadhana in India, and fifteen for the partnership with Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Those thirty are allocated. This site does not call them shipped.

About the count

The count is Flash Forward's own program record. Descriptions of each partner organization are on the partner pages, and those pages keep the partner's figures separate from ours.
