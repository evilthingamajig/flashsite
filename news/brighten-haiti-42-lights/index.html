Haiti · Brighten Haiti · 2026

42 study lights for Brighten Haiti

Flash Forward delivered 42 solar study lights to Haiti through its work with Brighten Haiti, adding an estimated 252 learning hours each day.

Brighten Haiti brings solar power to schools, hospitals, civic buildings and families that lack reliable electricity. Its work connects energy access with education, health care and locally led economic opportunity.

Its Solar4Schools program focuses on rural and underfunded schools. Brighten Haiti also trains Haitian solar apprentices to install, maintain and troubleshoot renewable-energy systems.

A study light is simple by design: it charges during the day and gives a student light for reading or homework at night. Brighten Haiti brings the local relationships to place each light with a student who can use it.
