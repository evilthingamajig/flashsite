Student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit

Together, we can bring light to study by

Flash Forward Foundation is a student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Bellevue, Washington. Our student interns build $5.17 solar study lights, and partner organizations give them to students who have no electricity after dark. As of October 2026, 72 lights have reached students in Haiti, India and the DR Congo.

Why light

Studying shouldn't stop at sunset

Without electricity, the school day effectively ends when the sun goes down. A small solar light gives a student a few more hours to read, revise and finish homework.

The team

Founded by students

Flash Forward began in 2024 as a student project in Bellevue. The IRS recognized the foundation as a 501(c)(3) public charity in 2025, and student interns still run it.
