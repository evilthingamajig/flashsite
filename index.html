“I have a piece of land that’s mine... no one is going to move me from there.”

Yamira Snchez
Ahualulco de Mercado

After 30 years of renting, Yamira watched her in-laws, then in their late sixties, lose their home with nowhere to go, and she decided her family’s future would be different.

She secured her own land, has already planted corn on it, and is working toward a home where her granddaughter Laila will finally have a room of her own.
