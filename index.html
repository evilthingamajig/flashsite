Flash Forward

Student-led light. Study hours unlocked.

We design solar study lights, partner for assembly and delivery, and put reliable evening light in the hands of learners without electricity.

Donate Join Get Involved

The problem

When the sun goes down, studying often stops.

Hundreds of millions of people still lack reliable electricity. For students, that can mean homework ends at dusk — lost hours that compound into lost opportunity.

Our model

The Study Unlock Pathway

Design → Assemble → Deliver → Unlock. Students lead design and operations. Assembly partners build. Distribution partners place lights with learners.

Design

Student-led design & ops

Interns run product, partnerships, and fundraising pipelines — not soldering lines.

Assemble

Assembly partners

Robotics clubs and makerspaces print, solder, and report dimensions. Flash Forward supplies key parts.

Deliver

Distribution partners

Local orgs with student access place lights and help us learn what works.

Unlock

Study impact

Modeled study hours unlocked — with a transparent method, not inflated claims.
