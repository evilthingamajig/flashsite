We design, build, and deliver solar study lights so students can keep learning after dark.

Our purpose
Flash Forward Foundation

A useful idea becomes meaningful when people put it to work. Our student-led team connects engineering, hands-on building, and local distribution to make solar study lights available to students.

As of October 2026, 72 lights had been delivered through Brighten Haiti, Loksadhana in India, and Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Each delivery is a step toward more students having light to study.
