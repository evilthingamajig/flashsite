Student-run · Bellevue, WA

Together, We Can Bring Light to Study By

Flash Forward Foundation is a student-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Bellevue, Washington. Volunteers build $5.17 solar study lights for students who have no electricity to study after dark.

From workshop to student

How it works

1

Build in Bellevue

Student volunteers print the case, solder the electronics and assemble every light.

2

Ship to partners

Finished lights are wrapped, packed and sent to partners in three countries.

3

Study after dark

A charge in daylight gives up to about 12 hours of reading light after sunset.

Learn how a light is built →

The study light

$5.17 in parts

Six parts, assembled by student volunteers who build for free. Shipping is separate.

  • ~7 hours of sunlight to charge
  • Up to ~12 hours of light per charge
  • Built to last 1–2 years
