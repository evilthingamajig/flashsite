Studying shouldn’t stop at sunset.

A student-led nonprofit putting solar study lights in the hands of students who have no electricity after dark.

The Study Unlock Pathway: design, assemble, deliver, and unlock study hours

Design

Students run the organization

Student interns design the light and run partnerships, operations, fundraising and marketing.

Assemble

Assembly partners build

Robotics clubs and makerspaces 3D-print the case and solder each light from parts we send them.

Deliver

Distribution partners deliver

Local organizations that already work with schools hand the lights to students who need them.

“The solar system is not just light, it’s our hope for the future in Dumas.”

Tamisade, NS2
Dumas, Haiti

Tamisade is one of 26 students at Institution Sainte Bernadette Soubirous de Dumas who wrote about school before and after solar power, for an essay contest run by our partner Brighten Haiti.

42 study lights have gone to students through Brighten Haiti, so the light can follow them home. Read the essays.
