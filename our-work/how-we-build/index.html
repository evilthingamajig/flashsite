The build

How a Light Is Built

Each light is designed, assembled and packed by student volunteers in Bellevue, Washington. The steps below follow one light from parts to the shipping box.

Step 1

Solder the electronics

The TP4056 charge board, LiPo battery and the two 5 mm LEDs are soldered together. The board controls charging and protects the battery.

Step 2

Assemble the case

The 3D-printed case, 5 V solar panel lid and slide switch go together. The switch slides between off, charge and light.

Step 3

Test every unit

Each finished light is switched on and checked. Only lights that pass go into the shipment.

Step 4

Pack and ship

Lights are bubble-wrapped and boxed for the trip to the partner organization, which places each light with a student.
