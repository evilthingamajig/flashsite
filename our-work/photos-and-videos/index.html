Photos and Videos

Student volunteers build every Flash Forward light in Bellevue, Washington. These photos and videos show the workshop, the parts and the finished lights.

Assembling a study light. A volunteer fits and connects the components on a workbench. Tools, wiring, the case, and the solar panel are visible as the light is assembled.
Printing the case. The 3D printer moves across the print bed, laying down the material for a light’s enclosure.
A closer look at the light. A hand holds and turns a prototype at a desk, showing the solar panel and case, then switches on the LEDs.
Meeting the student team. Visitors and students gather around a table during a foundation presentation. The team discusses the project and displays its work.
