Brighten Haiti

Brighten Haiti brings solar power to schools, hospitals, civic buildings and families that lack reliable electricity. 42 lights have shipped to this partner.

Our Work with Brighten Haiti

Its work connects energy access with education, health care, and locally led economic opportunity.

Solar4Schools focuses on rural and underfunded schools. Brighten Haiti also trains Haitian solar apprentices to install, maintain, and troubleshoot renewable-energy systems, building local skills alongside the infrastructure.

About the Partnership

Why the local partnership matters

A study light is intentionally simple. It charges during the day and gives a student a dependable source of light for reading, homework, or another task at night. Brighten Haiti brings local relationships and solar experience built through its work with schools and communities across Haiti.

That local foundation helps the lights reach students through an organization already working to expand reliable energy access.

What Flash Forward contributes

Flash Forward is a volunteer-run nonprofit that designs and builds rechargeable solar study lights. Young volunteers contribute through engineering, assembly, fundraising, media, and operations. The finished lights connect that hands-on work with students who need another option for learning after dark.
