About Brighten Haiti

About Brighten Haiti

Brighten Haiti brings solar power to schools, hospitals, civic buildings and families that lack reliable electricity. Its Solar4Schools program focuses on rural and underfunded schools, and it trains Haitian solar apprentices to install and maintain renewable-energy systems.

The partnership began with the 42-light shipment; Brighten Haiti’s school relationships place each light with a student.

The partnership

How the lights reach students

Flash Forward provides the lights to Brighten Haiti free of charge. Brighten Haiti chooses which students receive each light — the partner knows the students and households. Student volunteers in Bellevue build the lights; the partner puts them to work.
