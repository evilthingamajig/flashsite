About Malaika

About Malaika

Malaika was founded in 2007 in Kalebuka, near Lubumbashi. Its free school for girls opened in 2011 and reports 430 girls enrolled with 99.3% retention. A community center serves the wider village.

The partnership

How the lights reach students

Flash Forward provides the lights to Malaika free of charge. Malaika chooses which students receive each light — the partner knows the students and households. Student volunteers in Bellevue build the lights; the partner puts them to work.
