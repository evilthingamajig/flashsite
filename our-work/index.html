Our Work

What we build and where it goes

Flash Forward Foundation designs, builds and delivers $5.17 solar study lights for students without electricity. Student interns build each light in Bellevue, Washington, and partner organizations in Haiti, India and the DR Congo give them to students. The four pages below cover the light, the build, the partners and the photos.
