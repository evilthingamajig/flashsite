The Study Light

One light. Six parts. $5.17.

The Flash Forward study light is a small solar desk light that student interns build for $5.17 in parts: a printed case, a ~500 mAh battery, a charge board, two LEDs, a slide switch and a solar panel. About 7 hours of sunlight gives up to about 12 hours of light, and each light is built to last 1–2 years.
