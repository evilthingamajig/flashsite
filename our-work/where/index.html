FAQ

Questions

Where do the lights go?

Flash Forward has delivered 72 lights to three partners: 42 to Brighten Haiti in Haiti, 15 to Loksadhana in Maharashtra, India, and 15 to Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Have all the lights been delivered?

Yes. All 72 lights have been delivered to the three partners.

Who chooses which students get a light?

The partner organization chooses recipients. It knows the students and communities it serves.

Can a donor pick a destination?

Yes — on the donation page you can prefer one of the three partner countries or let Flash Forward choose where it's needed most.
