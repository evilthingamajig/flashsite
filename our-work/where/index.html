Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the lights go?

Flash Forward has delivered 72 lights to three partners: 42 lights to Brighten Haiti in Haiti, 15 lights to Loksadhana in Maharashtra, India, and 15 lights to Malaika in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Have all the lights been delivered?

Yes. All 72 lights have been delivered to the three partners.

Why do lights go through partners?

Partner organizations already work with schools and communities. They know the students and households and choose who receives each light.

What does one light cost?

The parts for one light cost $5.17. Student volunteers build the lights without pay. Shipping is separate. By our estimate, a light is used about 6 hours a day.

Partner Record

Lights are built in Bellevue, Washington. This record lists each partner and the lights it has received.

Lights delivered

Brighten Haiti (Haiti): 42 lights delivered. Brighten Haiti brings solar power to schools, hospitals and families across Haiti through Solar4Schools. Learn More

Loksadhana (India): 15 lights delivered. A charitable trust in Maharashtra advancing rural education, women’s empowerment and health. Learn More

Malaika (DR Congo): 15 lights delivered. Malaika empowers girls and communities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo through education, health and clean water. Learn More

Other partners

Flash Forward also works with these organizations.

Rethread Global is a social enterprise creating dignified employment for people rebuilding their lives.
